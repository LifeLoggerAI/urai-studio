## Command-center responsibilities

URAI Studio presents one truthful production graph:

`canon -> sequence -> scene -> shot -> asset -> render -> review -> editorial master -> accessibility master -> immersive delivery`

## Required release profiles

- local-proof
- storyboard
- full-animatic
- proof-chapter
- feature-production
- final-post
- clearance
- immersive-derivatives

Each profile defines required integrations, gates, evidence, budgets, roles, and publication boundaries.

## Required review surfaces

- canon and factual confidence
- consent and privacy
- character/age continuity
- location/prop/wardrobe continuity
- cinematography and performance
- music, Foley, dialogue, and mix
- captions, transcript, audio description, haptics, sensory-safe alternatives
- rights, likeness, trademark, archive, and location clearance
- provider spend and retained provenance
- version comparison and rejection reasons

## Advancement rule

A shot cannot advance when required canon, consent, source, continuity, rights, budget, accessibility, or review fields are missing. Configuration alone is never reported as integration health.

## Private evidence

Private family likeness and source evidence remains access-controlled in Drive. Public/demo Studio surfaces use redacted metadata, opaque IDs, approved proxies, or synthetic fixtures.

## Proof chapter

The Farm-to-Lake chapter must be operable end-to-end from this command center, including no-spend deterministic proof, storyboard, animatic, authorized final candidates, review, evidence retention, and handoff to Spatial.

This document establishes the contract only; it does not claim production integrations are live or the feature is complete.
